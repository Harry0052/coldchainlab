# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First rehearsal release.

- One synthetic refrigerator, a configurable logger, and three fault kinds: power loss, door open, and logger dropout.
- The bundled power-loss drill reports a 3 minute detection delay.
- The dropout replay reports an undetected excursion and a separate gap alert.
- Scenario and result export as JSON, CSV, and a text summary. Thresholds are demonstration values.
- Fixtures are original closed-form series. NIST IR 7656 and the CDC Vaccine Storage and Handling Toolkit are linked as method context and are not redistributed.
